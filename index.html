About Cortex Vault

Cortex Vault is a small collection of free brain teasers, logic puzzles, memory drills, and short deduction cases. Everything is instant — no account, no email, no download. Answer a few questions and see your result right away.

The tests here are built for fun and self-reflection, not clinical or professional assessment. They're a quick way to pass a few minutes and see how your mind tends to work — nothing more, nothing less.

New tests and mystery cases are added over time. If you have an idea for one, get in touch on the contact page.
